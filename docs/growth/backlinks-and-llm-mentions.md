## LLM mentions (GEO) — the playbook

**The bombshell finding: syllary.com's homepage is invisible to every AI assistant.** AI crawlers (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) execute **zero JavaScript**; the homepage is a Vite SPA shell that serves only a `<title>` without JS — and that title says "synced lyric files," underselling video. The Cloudflare SEO worker only injects content on `/p/*` and `/convert|tools|compare|guides/*`. **Everything else in this playbook is throttled until an assistant can read what Syllary is.**

### Site work (Claude implements)
1. **P0 — Extend infra/seo-worker to serve full definitional HTML on `/` and `/pricing`**: "Syllary is an AI lyric-video maker that turns an uploaded MP3/WAV/FLAC into a synced, AI-painted 1080p music video…" + features, pricing table, FAQ, SoftwareApplication + FAQPage JSON-LD (pattern exists in seo-landing-page.tsx).
2. **P0 — robots.txt: named Allow blocks** for OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended. (OpenAI docs: sites opted out of OAI-SearchBot cannot appear in ChatGPT search answers.) Then confirm Cloudflare bot-fight/WAF isn't 403ing these UAs.
3. **P1 — IndexNow pings** on landing-page publish (instant Bing indexing → ChatGPT).
4. **P1 — Competitor comparison pages** via the existing landing_pages system: vs LyricEdits, Neural Frames, Rotor, Specterr, SunoMV, VidMuse, One More Shot, Banger.Show — nobody contests "SunoMV alternative"-type queries yet. Honest tables, concede rival strengths, "Updated July 2026" stamps (fresh pages earn ~2x citations). ChatGPT cites vendor comparison pages at 20.1% of citations — this is disproportionately a ChatGPT lever.
5. **P1 — Own listicle pages**: "Best AI music video generator for Suno songs" — the SERP is 80% vendor-owned blogs already; our guide system can win it. (Freebeat + Neural Frames both run dedicated Suno-to-video pages; 2 of our first 3 payers came from our Suno guide.)
6. **P2 — llms.txt**: ship for ~30 min, expect nothing (97% of llms.txt files get zero requests; no major assistant confirmedly reads it).

### Per-engine map
| Engine | Index | Lever |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Bing (87% citation match) | Bing WMT + IndexNow + own comparison pages (cites vendor pages 20.1%) + listicles. Most niche-friendly engine. |
| Claude | Brave Search (86.7% match); searches only ~37% of prompts | Check ranking at search.brave.com; training-data presence matters most → allow GPTBot/Google-Extended etc. |
| Perplexity | Own crawler; never cites vendor pages (0.4%) | Third-party only: Reddit (its #1 source), listicles, review sites. |
| Gemini / AI Overviews | Google index | YouTube is the #1 cited source (~23%) — LLMs read transcripts, not videos. Classic SEO rank drives AIO. |

### YouTube (the Gemini lever)
3–5 videos: "Lyric video from your Suno song in 5 minutes," one per style, one honest comparison. Upload **corrected transcripts** + chapters; first two description lines = the definitional answer. Views don't matter for citations; transcripts do.

### Measurement
Run a **baseline this week, before the fixes ship**: 10 prompts monthly in fresh sessions of ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Google-with-AIO — "best way to make a lyric video for my Suno song," "best AI lyric video maker," "how do I turn a Suno song into a music video," "free lyric video maker no watermark," "app that syncs lyrics to my song and makes a video," "Syllary review," "Syllary alternatives," "LyricEdits alternatives," "best tool to make synced .lrc files," "AI music video generator for independent musicians." Log: mentioned?, position, cited sources (= next month's outreach list). Upgrade to Otterly.ai Lite $29/mo later. Engines agree on brands only ~21% of the time — track each separately. Expect first movement in ChatGPT/Perplexity 4–8 weeks after listicle+Reddit placements; Gemini slower; Claude last.
